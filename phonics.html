🔍 How the real toy works (peek inside)

The real toy is two tiny computers, no internet at all:

Helper 1 — the ears & mouth (M5Stack CoreS3, offline speech)
Helper 2 — the muscles (Arduino UNO Q: servos + lights)

They pass secret notes over a wire (UART). Watch the notes:

—

This page plays both helpers with your browser’s built-in speech. Heads-up: some browsers (like Chrome) send what you say to their own speech service to work out the words — the real toy never does that; it is fully offline.
