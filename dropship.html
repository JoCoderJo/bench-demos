The Trust & Safety Charter

Generic printable shops often scrape internet hacks or publish dangerous sleep gimmicks. We follow pediatric medical consensus word for word.

AAP 2022 Infant Safe Sleep

Every mention of baby sleep strictly follows the American Academy of Pediatrics: back to sleep, flat non-inclined surface, bare crib, and zero weighted sleepwear.

Rule: AAP 2022 • CPSC Safe Sleep Act

No Shaming • No Deadlines

Milestone printables celebrate moments, they never diagnose delays. We provide zero “should by” deadlines, linking directly to CDC Act Early checklists.

Rule: CDC Learn The Signs

Strict Child Privacy (COPPA)
