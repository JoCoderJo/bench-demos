# demo/audio/syllables — vowel teams said as one syllable (Piper, en_US-kristin-medium)

Generated by `tools/make-vowel-teams.py` from explicit phonemes (not text): ai/ay `ˈeɪ`, oa `ˈoʊ`, ow/ou `ˈaʊ`,
oi/oy `ˈɔɪ`, igh `ˈaɪ`, ar `ˈɑːɹ`, or `ˈoɹ`, er/ur/ir `ˈɚ`, aw `ˈɔː`. A vowel team is a whole syllable, so the
voice simply says it — unlike a consonant, where synthesis adds a vowel ("kuh") and breaks blending; that is why
the old `ipa/` consonant clips were retired from the tablet and why no consonant lives here.

`manifest.json` lists what was made and from which phonemes. Tier on the tablet (tablet/apps/letters/build.py):
Joe's recording > ShowAndTellLetter > approved Commons > **these** > same-sound alias. Record the real thing with
`tools/record-sounds.py` and the recording wins on the next build.
