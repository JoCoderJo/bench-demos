Application Protocol & Technique
6-step professional execution strategy
Step-by-Step
Application Order
1
Divide into four quadrants
Clean profile part from forehead to nape, and ear-to-ear through apex. Secure with duckbill clips.
2
Begin in the resistant areas
Start at the lower nape or densest zone where natural warmth is highest and lift develops slower.
3
Apply root formula
Deposit root melt cleanly on 1.5" regrowth. Feather downward with bevel comb to prevent hard demarcation.
4
Monitor lift
Check test foil at 15m and 25m. Clean off product with towel to check true level under balanced 5000K light.
5
Reassess mids before applying secondary formula
Verify how the mid-lengths are lifting before depositing secondary gloss to avoid muddy over-saturation.
6
Tone only after the desired underlying level is reached
Never tone prematurely over stubborn orange pigment. Toners deposit tone, they do not lift base depth.

Pro Technique Considerations

Saturation Guidelines: Full, wet saturation is mandatory. If lightener dries out on the hair strand, oxidation ceases and lifting stops.
When to Emulsify: In the final 3–5 minutes at the shampoo bowl, gently massage root shadow through mids to soften transition lines without drabness.
Suggested Toner Direction: Use blue-violet to neutralize orange-yellow brass, and beige-gold to maintain reflectiveness and prevent grey/hollow drabness.
When Multiple Sessions Are Needed: If natural starting level is 4 or darker and target is Level 8+, educate client on a 2-stage game plan to protect cuticle bonds.
The stylist remains the professional making the final decision.
